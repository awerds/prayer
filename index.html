MAKE ROOM FOR PRAYER

How would you like to listen?

Choose a voice for prayer. You can change this whenever you like.

The optional Kokoro voice downloads roughly 100 MB plus runtime files from Hugging Face and a code CDN. Speech is then generated locally. Downloaded model files are cached when the browser permits; storage may be cleared by your browser.

After each spoken line, a timed pause gives you room to respond. Longer lines receive more time, adjusted to your reading pace. The prayer continues automatically; Stop ends both speech and pauses.

Installed local voices are preferred. If none are available, your device’s default speech service is used; it may require a connection.

Your preferences and saved prayers stay in this browser. No account needed.
