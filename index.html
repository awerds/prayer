How would you like to listen?

Choose a voice for prayer. You can change this whenever you like.

KittenTTS 2 Pico int4 is an ultra-light neural voice model (4 MB int4 ONNX + 4 English voices + lexicon, ~9 MB total) that runs locally in your browser with synchronized lip movement. Cached locally once downloaded.

After each spoken line, a timed pause gives you room to respond. Longer lines receive more time, adjusted to your reading pace. The prayer continues automatically; Stop ends both speech and pauses.

Google US English is preferred when your browser offers it. Otherwise another US English voice or the device default is used. Google’s voice may require internet. Choose another voice below to override the default.

Your preferences and saved prayers stay in this browser. No account needed.
