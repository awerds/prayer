# The fifteen prayers of Saint Bridget

The complete fifteen prayers are taken from the public-domain 1874 *St. Bridget’s Manual*, published by Thomas Kelly, New York, as transcribed at https://saintsencyclopedia.com/prayers/15-prayers-of-st-bridget/ . Only the historical prayer text is reproduced; the modern article’s commentary and illustrations are not copied.

The transcription identifies the source as “The Prayers of St. Bridget. To be said in honor of the Blessed Wounds of our Divine Saviour.” Ora retains its sequence and wording. Each prayer is followed by the short invocation specified by that edition, then the app’s traditional Our Father and Hail Mary texts. Titles describing themes and usage notes are editorial additions. Wording and numbering differ from some modern Pietà prayer books.

The prayers are traditionally attributed to Saint Bridget; this is not a claim of certain authorship or approval of the promises sometimes attached to them. Historical language in the text is preserved as source material, not as an endorsement of collective blame against Jewish people.

The November 2026 [language review](PRAYER-REVIEW.md) adds paragraph breaks and visible editorial context without silently rewriting the historical text. The 1874 provenance is reported by the linked transcription; original printed pages have not been independently collated.
